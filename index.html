05 / Perspective

Frequently asked questions.

Direct, transparent answers regarding data safety, fine-tuning methodologies, and model ownership.

How much training data do we actually need? +
For targeted extraction, classification, or style adherence, high-quality curated sets of 500 to 2,500 domain examples often yield superior results compared to noisy million-token crawls. In our Discovery phase, we audit your raw samples and generate verified synthetic augmentations if your labeled corpus is small.
How do you ensure our data is never leaked or used elsewhere? +
Every client engagement runs inside an ephemeral, single-tenant cloud environment with encrypted volume storage. We execute bilateral Non-Disclosure and Data Processing Agreements under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. Training datasets are permanently wiped once weights are handed over.
When should we choose RAG versus Fine-Tuning? +
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is best for injecting dynamic factual knowledge, search queries, and recent documents. Fine-tuning is required when you need the model to learn a specific output syntax, complex categorization, specialized tone, or internalize domain reasoning patterns. The highest performing enterprise systems pair fine-tuned compact models with targeted RAG pipelines.
Which open-weight base models do you fine-tune? +
We primarily fine-tune Meta Llama (Llama 3.1 & 3.3 8B/70B), Qwen (Qwen 2.5 7B/14B/32B), Mistral/Mixtral, and Gemma 2. Depending on your latency constraints and target language, we benchmark multiple candidate models before selecting the production foundation.
Who owns the final fine-tuned model weights? +
You do. You retain 100% intellectual property ownership of the resulting adapter weights and checkpoint artifacts. You are free to run them on our hosted servers, take them into your private cloud, or host them on your own air-gapped on-premise hardware.
